## 10. What Should You Check Before Buying a Lakefront Apartment?

A lake view can be attractive, but buyers should evaluate the complete property rather than making a decision based only on the view.

Before purchasing a lakefront apartment in Kukatpally, consider:

### Location

Check the property's exact road access, surrounding development and connectivity.

### View

Understand which apartments actually have lake views and whether the view is permanent or dependent on surrounding development.

### Orientation

Compare the direction, natural light and ventilation of different units.

### Floor Plan

Check room sizes, balcony/deck space, privacy and circulation.

### Community Density

Compare the number of homes, towers and families sharing each floor.

### Amenities

Look beyond the number of amenities and check whether they are genuinely useful for your family.

### Approvals and RERA

Always verify the project's registration and approved plans before making a purchase decision.
